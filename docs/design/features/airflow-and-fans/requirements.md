# Requirements: Airflow and fans: the exact steady solver and its analyses

The Airflow group of the App: the steady network solver of the browser (`frontend/src/engine/`), its offline reference (`data-pipeline/aerovia_pipeline/model.py`), fan duty, the common-speed optimiser, the speed sweep and the resistance sensitivity. Written on 2026-10-20 after the code (retroactive).

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, doi:10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated.

| ID | Requirement | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| AF-001 | THE solver SHALL reproduce the closed-form series solution (flow, pressure, velocity and electrical power) and the closed-form parallel split of flow. | `frontend/src/engine.test.ts::matches the series resistance, pressure, velocity and electrical-power solution` and `frontend/src/engine.test.ts::matches parallel resistance and flow distribution` |
| AF-002 | THE solver SHALL obey the common-speed laws, flow with speed, pressure with its square and fan power with its cube, and WHILE the fan is off THE solver SHALL return exactly zero flow and zero power. | `frontend/src/engine.test.ts::obeys common-speed flow, pressure and fan cube laws` and `frontend/src/engine.test.ts::has exactly zero flow and zero power with the fan off` |
| AF-003 | THE solver SHALL conserve flow at a dead end and in a zero-flow branch, and under branch regulation and target and area interventions on every authored case. | `frontend/src/engine.test.ts::conserves at a dead end and a zero-flow branch` and `frontend/src/engine.test.ts::maintains conservation under branch regulation and target/area interventions` |
| AF-004 | IF airway closures isolate internal nodes, THEN THE solver SHALL return an explicit failure, and THE solver SHALL not treat a closed targeted working as target-compliant. | `frontend/src/engine.test.ts::returns an explicit failure if airway closures isolate internal nodes` and `frontend/src/engine.test.ts::does not treat a closed targeted working as target-compliant` and `tests/python/test_model.py::test_closure_isolation_rejected_without_arbitrary_pressure_anchor` |
| AF-005 | IF a network has zero resistance, duplicate identifiers, unknown endpoints, absent boundaries, floating nodes or an unsupported size, THEN THE solver SHALL reject it. | `frontend/src/engine.test.ts::rejects zero resistance, duplicate IDs, unknown endpoints and absent boundaries` and `frontend/src/engine.test.ts::rejects floating imported nodes and unsupported sizes` and `tests/python/test_model.py::test_invalid_networks_rejected` |
| AF-006 | IF an option is invalid, THEN THE solver SHALL reject it and SHALL not clamp a scientific input. | `frontend/src/engine.test.ts::rejects invalid options rather than clamping scientific inputs` and `tests/python/test_model.py::test_invalid_options_rejected` |
| AF-007 | WHEN a fan runs in reverse or delivers a negative pressure, THE solver SHALL flag it and SHALL not report useful fan power. | `frontend/src/engine.test.ts::flags reverse fan operation instead of inventing useful fan power` and `frontend/src/engine.test.ts::flags forward fan operation with negative delivered pressure` and `tests/python/test_model.py::test_reverse_fan_is_explicit_unsupported_state` |
| AF-008 | THE optimiser SHALL find the lowest common fan speed in the range zero to 1.5 that meets every target, SHALL report infeasible targets as infeasible, and SHALL decline to assert a monotone optimum when the boundary pressures are unequal. | `frontend/src/engine.test.ts::finds the known minimum feasible common fan speed` and `frontend/src/engine.test.ts::reports infeasible targets and no-target zero demand` and `frontend/src/engine.test.ts::does not assert a monotone optimum with unequal boundary pressures` |
| AF-009 | THE sensitivity analysis SHALL match a closed-form perturbation, and THE speed sweep SHALL be generated from actual solutions including zero and the maximum speed. | `frontend/src/engine.test.ts::computes resistance sensitivity against a closed-form perturbation` and `frontend/src/engine.test.ts::generates a fan sweep from actual solutions including zero and maximum speed` |
| AF-010 | THE browser solver SHALL solve a connected network at 120 junctions and 240 airways. | `frontend/src/engine.test.ts::solves a connected network at the 120-node / 240-edge browser limits` |
| AF-011 | THE browser solver SHALL reproduce each of the 12 committed reference results (flows, pressures, fan power, intake and target ratio) within the tolerances of the method ladder in the SDD. | `frontend/src/engine.test.ts::requires a complete numerical reference artifact` and (the per-case tests of the same file, 'matches independent flow, pressure and power' for each case, hold the tolerances) |
| AF-012 | THE offline reference SHALL reproduce the analytic series and parallel solutions with electrical power and equivalent resistance. | `tests/python/test_model.py::test_analytic_series_flow_pressure_and_electric_power` and `tests/python/test_model.py::test_analytic_parallel_split_and_equivalent_resistance` |
| AF-013 | THE fan plot SHALL not display a delivered pressure for a closed fan branch, SHALL not infer a quadratic system curve from unequal imposed boundary pressures, and SHALL not use a stale result of another airway count. | `frontend/src/components/FanPlot.test.ts::does not display delivered pressure for a closed fan branch` and `frontend/src/components/FanPlot.test.ts::does not infer a quadratic system curve from unequal imposed boundary pressures` and `frontend/src/components/FanPlot.test.ts::cannot use a stale result with the wrong number of airways` |
