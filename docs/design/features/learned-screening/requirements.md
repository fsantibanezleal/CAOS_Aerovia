# Requirements: Learned screening: the two learned methods and their exports

The Learned models group of the App and the learned pipeline behind it (`data-pipeline/aerovia_pipeline/surrogate_*.py`, `frontend/src/learned/`): the topology MLP and the shared graph network, their conservation projection, splits, domain checks, training state, ONNX exports and registry. Written on 2026-10-20 after the code (retroactive).

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, doi:10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated.

| ID | Requirement | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| LS-001 | THE learned post-processing SHALL leave an exact reference flow unchanged under the conservation projection, and SHALL reconstruct pressures that match the nonlinear reference. | `tests/python/test_surrogates.py::test_conservation_projector_is_identity_on_independent_reference` and `tests/python/test_surrogates.py::test_pressure_reconstruction_matches_nonlinear_reference` |
| LS-002 | THE dataset split SHALL be reproducible from its seeds and SHALL keep the training, validation, calibration and test seeds disjoint. | `tests/python/test_surrogates.py::test_split_seeds_are_reproducible_and_disjoint` |
| LS-003 | IF a state is outside the trained domain (an unknown topology, a closure, a curve edit or an extrapolation), THEN THE learned lane SHALL refuse it explicitly, in the pipeline and in the browser. | `tests/python/test_surrogates.py::test_domain_rejects_actual_unsupported_state` and `frontend/src/learned/domain.test.ts::rejects unknown topology, closure, curve edit and extrapolation explicitly` |
| LS-004 | THE learned lane SHALL not feed area or target edits into the hydraulic features, and SHALL not turn them into invented hydraulic inputs. | `tests/python/test_surrogates.py::test_area_and_target_do_not_enter_hydraulic_feature_path` and `frontend/src/learned/domain.test.ts::area and target edits do not become invented hydraulic inputs` |
| LS-005 | THE learned lane SHALL not present a perturbed output as a converged physical solution, and SHALL return zero flow, pressure and power at zero speed without fabricated inference. | `frontend/src/learned/domain.test.ts::does not present a perturbed output as a converged physical solution` and `frontend/src/learned/domain.test.ts::zero-speed arrays have zero flow, pressure and power without fabricated inference` |
| LS-006 | THE graph network SHALL be equivariant to a permutation of nodes and edges, and the learned prediction SHALL change with the resistance without calling the solver. | `tests/python/test_surrogates.py::test_gnn_is_equivariant_to_node_and_edge_permutation` and `tests/python/test_surrogates.py::test_neural_prediction_changes_with_resistance_without_solver` |
| LS-007 | THE training SHALL resume from a checkpoint with the same optimiser, scheduler and random state it stopped with. | `tests/python/test_surrogates.py::test_resume_preserves_training_state_exactly` |
| LS-008 | EVERY exported ONNX file SHALL have the input and output tensors its registry entry declares, and the registry, the model manifest and the networks SHALL agree. | `tests/contracts/test_models.py::test_every_onnx_file_has_the_io_its_registry_entry_declares` and `tests/contracts/test_models.py::test_an_onnx_file_whose_io_differs_from_its_entry_fails` and `tests/contracts/test_models.py::test_registry_entries_agree_with_the_manifest_and_the_networks` |
| LS-009 | THE checkpoint reader SHALL reject a checkpoint with a missing key and SHALL refuse a hostile pickle. | `tests/contracts/test_models.py::test_a_checkpoint_missing_a_key_is_rejected_and_a_hostile_pickle_refused` |
| LS-010 | THE App SHALL label a learned field as an approximation and SHALL reject a changed topology without substituting a fabricated output. | `frontend/e2e/workbench.spec.ts::learned fields are labeled approximations and reject a changed topology without substituting fabricated output` |
