# Requirements: Tracer transport: conservative passive transport and directed routes

The Transport group of the App: the mixed-cell passive-tracer integrator (`frontend/src/engine/transport.ts`), its worker and the directed-route analysis (`frontend/src/engine/routing.ts`). Written on 2026-10-20 after the code (retroactive).

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, doi:10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated.

| ID | Requirement | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| TT-001 | THE integrator SHALL match the exponential washout of one mixed volume and SHALL close the tracer ledger (injected equals stored plus escaped plus removed) independently of the integrator. | `frontend/src/engine/transport.test.ts::matches one mixed volume exponential washout and independently closes the ledger` |
| TT-002 | THE integrator SHALL converge at second order in time against the exact mixed-volume solution. | `frontend/src/engine/transport.test.ts::converges at second order in time against the exact mixed-volume solution` |
| TT-003 | THE integrator SHALL match the independent Erlang survival expression for equal serial cells. | `frontend/src/engine/transport.test.ts::matches the independent Erlang survival expression for four airway cells` |
| TT-004 | THE integrator SHALL mix incoming tracer flux at an internal junction without duplicating tracer, and SHALL split outgoing flux in proportion to the actual airflow. | `frontend/src/engine/transport.test.ts::mixes incoming flux at an internal junction without duplicating tracer` and `frontend/src/engine/transport.test.ts::splits outgoing tracer flux according to actual airflow` |
| TT-005 | THE integrator SHALL treat a pressure boundary as a clean external reservoir: incoming tracer SHALL leave the model and SHALL not be recycled into outgoing air. | `frontend/src/engine/transport.test.ts::exports incoming tracer at a pressure boundary rather than recycling it into outgoing air` |
| TT-006 | WHILE an airway is closed or its flow is zero, THE integrator SHALL retain its tracer and account for decay, and WHEN the airway reopens it SHALL resume transport at the exact reopening event. | `frontend/src/engine/transport.test.ts::retains tracer while closed and resumes transport at the exact reopening event` and `frontend/src/engine/transport.test.ts::retains trapped mass in a closed branch and accounts for decay with zero airflow` |
| TT-007 | THE integrator SHALL give a physically identical result under edge reversal with the source position mapped, and SHALL place a source on a cell interface symmetrically under orientation reversal. | `frontend/src/engine/transport.test.ts::is physically identical under edge reversal with the source position mapped` and `frontend/src/engine/transport.test.ts::places a source on a cell interface symmetrically under orientation reversal` |
| TT-008 | THE integrator SHALL reject an excessive integration workload before stepping, and SHALL reject invalid source references, dimensions, ranges and late events, never changing an input silently. | `frontend/src/engine/transport.test.ts::rejects excessive integration work before stepping instead of silently changing inputs` and `frontend/src/engine/transport.test.ts::rejects invalid source references, dimensions, ranges and late events` |
| TT-009 | THE integrator SHALL reject a schedule that changes the stored volume and an ambiguous simultaneous operating state, and SHALL refuse an unsupported disconnected airflow instead of generating a concentration. | `frontend/src/engine/transport.test.ts::rejects volume-changing schedules and ambiguous simultaneous operating states` and `frontend/src/engine/transport.test.ts::refuses unsupported disconnected airflow instead of generating a fabricated concentration` |
| TT-010 | THE integrator SHALL align a pulse exactly to an output time without smearing its injection, and SHALL integrate a finite continuous release and its washout without extending the source. | `frontend/src/engine/transport.test.ts::aligns a pulse exactly to an output time and does not smear its injection` and `frontend/src/engine/transport.test.ts::integrates finite continuous release and subsequent washout without extending the source` |
| TT-011 | THE route analysis SHALL choose the least nominal advective transit even when that route is geometrically longer, and SHALL orient airways by the solved flow and not by their declared orientation. | `frontend/src/engine/routing.test.ts::chooses least nominal advective transit even when that route is geometrically longer` and `frontend/src/engine/routing.test.ts::uses flow direction rather than edge declaration orientation` |
| TT-012 | THE route analysis SHALL stop at an external pressure boundary, SHALL never report a cycle through a clean external reservoir as internal recirculation, and SHALL give a closed or stagnant airway no finite travel time. | `frontend/src/engine/routing.test.ts::terminates an air parcel at an external pressure boundary` and `frontend/src/engine/routing.test.ts::never reports a cycle through a clean external reservoir as internal recirculation` and `frontend/src/engine/routing.test.ts::represents closed and stagnant airways without fictitious finite travel times` |
