# Narrow-vein inclined mine

ID `narrow-incline`; 36 nodes, 45 airways. Three production horizons are shifted horizontally in proportion to depth, creating an oblique network. Working resistances are multiplied by 2.4 plus the level index; working areas shrink from 8 to 6 m², and targets are 12 m³/s.

Inspect the geometry with level separation off first, then separate the horizons for selection. Compare working velocity, signed flow and target ratio; smaller area can increase velocity even when available volume falls. Modify a deep working's resistance and compare its sensitivity with changes to a shared shaft.

The inclination is a presentation of the authored geometry. Resistance values are explicit independent assumptions, and the model excludes natural ventilation pressure despite the vertical layout.
