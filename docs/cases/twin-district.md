# Asymmetric production districts

ID `twin-district`; 34 nodes, 42 airways. A shared intake hub and exhaust fan serve five working circuits west and five east. The compact western resistance multiplier is 0.7 and the longer eastern multiplier is 1.7. Each working target is 20 m³/s.

Save a baseline and compare forward production flows across the two arms. Increase the entrance resistance of the western arm and inspect whether eastern branches gain flow. Compare total fan power and the minimum target ratio; a redistribution intervention can have a different objective from maximizing total intake. The paired `district-regulation` case records a specific entrance resistance change.

The asymmetry is an explicit engineering input. Arm direction, visual length and map labels do not automatically determine resistance or measured productivity.
